Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Oddly Explained Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of its YouTube channels, on their own computer, to upload and schedule their own videos.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool asks for one permission: to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope). It does not read your email, contacts, files, viewing history or any other Google data.

What is stored

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, so the tool can upload the owner's scheduled videos. Nothing is stored on any server run by this tool.

What is shared

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Videos and their titles, descriptions and thumbnails are sent only to YouTube, to the owner's own channel.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account: Security, then "Your connections to third-party apps & services", then remove "Oddly Explained Uploader".

Google API Services

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
